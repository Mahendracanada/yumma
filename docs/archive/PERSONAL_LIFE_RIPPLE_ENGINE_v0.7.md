# Yumma Personal Life Ripple Engine — v0.7

## Purpose
The Life Ripple Engine maps a request to possible consequences across connected life domains, then offers proportionate, non-executing guidance. It is a transparent deterministic rule engine over synthetic inputs, not a predictive model or a claim that harm has occurred.

## Domains represented
- Financial loss and essential-needs impact
- Account security and digital identity
- Device control, privacy and device security
- Support network
- Decision quality and emotional pressure

## Graph behaviour
1. Request cues activate consequence rules.
2. Each activated rule emits a domain, severity, possible consequence and suggested intervention.
3. Relevant active Personal Wisdom Graph boundaries and commitments may be surfaced as context; the engine never edits those memories.
4. The response posture is proportionate: `pause_and_protect`, `verify_without_alarm`, or `informational_only`.
5. Policy and tool permissions remain independently controlled by the deterministic policy layer. Life Ripple cannot authorize an action.

## Guardrails
- No claim that a consequence is certain or has occurred.
- No external action, contacting supporters, financial execution or account control.
- Rules are explainable and reviewable; a missing rule is not proof of safety.
- Synthetic demonstration only; not independently validated for real-world detection performance.

## v0.7 verification
Five focused tests cover multi-domain payment pressure, remote access consequences, linkage to relevant personal wisdom, benign informational requests, and the presence of concrete interventions. Full automated suite: 36/36 passed in the local environment.
