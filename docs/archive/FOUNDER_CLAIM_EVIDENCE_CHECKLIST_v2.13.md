## Evidence handling

- Redact personal identifiers, private contact details, compensation, client confidential information and unrelated third-party information before sharing documents.
- Do not upload confidential employer programme reports unless disclosure is authorised. A short employer-approved reference or redacted extract is preferable.
- Keep a copy of each source and record which exact slide/script sentence it supports.
- Distinguish “led”, “sponsored”, “advised”, “contributed to” and “participated in”; use only the verb the evidence supports.

## Safe founder narration if documentary evidence is not ready

“I bring senior experience across banking, technology and transformation. That perspective has shaped Yumma’s focus on trust, human control and practical protection. I am applying that experience to a working synthetic prototype, and I am careful to distinguish what is implemented today from what still needs validation.”

This avoids unverified exact budgets, percentages, awards and employer endorsements while retaining the founder's relevant professional context.
