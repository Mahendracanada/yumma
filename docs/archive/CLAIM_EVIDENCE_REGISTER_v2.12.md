# Yumma competition deck — claim evidence register (v2.12)

This register records the external market claims checked for the 15-slide competition deck. It does not independently verify founder credentials or private project history.

## Slide 4 — UAE scam exposure (2024 study)

- **56%** of UAE survey respondents encountered a scam attempt at least once a month.
- **27%** reported losing money; average loss reported as **US$2,194 / AED 8,058**.
- **49%** of scams were completed within 24 hours of first contact.
- **9%** of victims fully recovered losses.
- **69%** realised themselves they had been scammed; **21%** were notified by banks.

Evidence: UAE Government Cybersecurity Council and TRENDS Research & Advisory study, conducted with GASA and BioCatch, about 1,964 UAE respondents. WAM's 28 Nov 2024 release confirms the 56%, 27%, US$2,194, 49%, 9%, 69% and 21% figures. Khaleej Times' 28 Nov 2024 report gives the AED 8,058 conversion. The deck now says **survey respondents** rather than generalising the sample to all residents.

- WAM: https://www.wam.ae/a/b6f93te
- Khaleej Times: https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/over-55-of-uae-residents-targeted-by-scams-monthly-shopping-fraud-identity-theft-most-common

## Slide 5 — Demand signals (Visa Stay Secure UAE study)

- **92%** worry that family or friends might fall for a scam.
- **49%** have fallen for a scam; **15%** were victims more than once.
- **62%** have been approached by someone requesting a money transfer on their behalf.
- **99%** actively take precautions to secure online transactions.
- **61%** decline emails asking them to transfer money for someone else; **59%** refrain from sharing card or account credentials.

Evidence: Visa press release dated 18 Mar 2025, reporting its 2025 Stay Secure UAE study commissioned from Wakefield Research and conducted in December 2024. Visa says the UAE sample was 300 adults within a 5,800-adult, 17-market CEMEA study. Slide 5's weaker, indirectly sourced IDEMIA 63% / 32% claim and its inadequately matched Dubai Police / The National citation have been removed from the updated deck.

- Visa primary source: https://www.visa.com/en-ae/newsroom/uae-digital-payments-security-consumer-study
