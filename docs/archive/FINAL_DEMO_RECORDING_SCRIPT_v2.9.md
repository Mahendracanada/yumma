# Yumma — final two-minute live-agent recording script

**Target length:** 1:55–1:58. **Hard ceiling:** 2:00.  
**Format:** actual browser agent + participant webcam overlay + spoken explanation.  
**Data:** synthetic demonstration only.

## Spoken script and screen cues

**0:00–0:12 | Welcome screen**  
“Meet Yumma, a personal life-intelligence AI agent. She helps people pause, understand pressure and make their own decisions.”

**0:12–0:28 | Run bank-transfer-pressure**  
“This is a synthetic bank-impersonation request. The caller creates urgency, asks for a transfer and tells the person to keep it secret.”

**0:28–0:55 | Show the actual result**  
“Yumma connects the signals and moves to Pause and Protect. She explains why independent verification matters. This is a deterministic local prototype—not a live bank connection.”

**0:55–1:12 | Show policy and audit evidence**  
“The safety policy does not authorise a transfer or any external action. The person remains in control. A remembered commitment can guide a pause, but never grants authority.”

**1:12–1:32 | Run benign-information**  
“Yumma also needs to avoid unnecessary alarm. Here, a benign banking information request stays informational rather than triggering a high-risk intervention.”

**1:32–1:52 | Show evidence slide/card**  
“The local verification run passes 64 automated tests and all four synthetic judge scenarios. These are engineering tests, not real-world fraud accuracy or independent security certification.”

**1:52–2:00 | Closing screen**  
“Yumma helps people keep their choices, their dignity and their independence.”

## Recording direction
- Keep the webcam overlay visible in a corner throughout; do not obscure the scenario result.
- Use the actual interface and perform the two scenario runs on camera.
- Use the participant's own voice; do not substitute generated narration for the participant's required explanation.
- Export MP4, verify playback and confirm duration is no more than 120 seconds.
